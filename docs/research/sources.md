## Research note template

Copy this structure into a new note when researching a question:

- **Question:** What are we trying to find out?
- **Source and date:** Original URL, publication date if available, and date accessed.
- **Finding:** What does the source actually establish?
- **Interpretation:** What might this mean for our game?
- **Limitations:** What remains unknown or untested?
- **Next action:** What evidence would help us decide?

## Evidence labels

**Documented:** supported by a linked primary source. **Proposed:** an idea or method to evaluate. **Tested:** supported by a recorded experiment. **Decided:** accepted for the project with a reason in the decision log.
