## One-page concept template

### Player experience

What should the player feel? Who is the game for? Where will they play it?

### Core loop

The player repeatedly **does an action**, receives **feedback**, and makes **a meaningful choice** to pursue **a goal**.

### First version

Name one environment, one central mechanic, one success condition, and one failure or retry condition.

### AI's role

Which development tasks could AI help with? Does the game itself need a model at runtime? What will we verify ourselves?

### Scope boundary

List the features we are deliberately postponing. Multiplayer, large worlds, complex inventories, and generated dialogue should each earn their place through a clear player benefit.

### Evidence needed

What would convince us this concept is feasible and worth prototyping?
